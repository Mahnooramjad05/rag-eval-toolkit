# Northwind Analytics: plans and pricing

Northwind Analytics is a fictional product used for demos. It offers three plans: Starter, Team and Enterprise.

The Starter plan costs 29 dollars per month and includes 3 user seats and 10 dashboards. It is billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time.

The Team plan costs 99 dollars per month and includes 15 user seats, unlimited dashboards and scheduled email reports. Annual billing on the Team plan gives a 20 percent discount.

The Enterprise plan has custom pricing. It adds single sign-on, a dedicated account manager and a 99.95 percent uptime commitment in the service level agreement.

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start the trial, and trial workspaces are deleted 30 days after the trial ends if no plan is chosen.

Refunds are available within 30 days of the first payment on annual plans. Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months.
