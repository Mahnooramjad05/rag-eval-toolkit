# Northwind Analytics: support

Starter customers get email support with a first response within 2 business days.

Team customers get email and chat support with a first response within 8 business hours.

Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone support and a first response within 1 hour for critical incidents.

The status page at status.northwind.example shows current incidents and planned maintenance. Planned maintenance is announced at least 72 hours in advance.

Onboarding sessions are included free with annual Team plans and all Enterprise plans.
